## Problem

A three-week period ends with a question only the Product Owner can answer:
is the Increment accepted. Whether each Sprint Goal is met is already
decided by evidence: track-goals has recorded which tests passed in which
environment on which version. What evidence cannot decide is whether the
result is what the users needed. Reviewed from a demo alone, the Increment
is accepted on impression; reviewed from closed issues, it is accepted on
activity.

The sprint-review skill shows the evidence, asks the Product Owner for the
verdict, and pushes every gap the review names as a follow-up item.

<!-- field: goals -->
## Goals

- G1: The Increment is judged against recorded evidence, not against closed issues.
- G2: Acceptance stays a Product Owner judgment, separate from met.
- G3: Every gap the review names becomes an item, not a note.

## R6 - Direction

<!-- req: R6.1 -->
### R6.1

At a period's end, sprint-review shows the Product Owner each Sprint Goal's ledger and evidence, records the Product Owner's verdict on the Increment, and pushes follow-up items.

<!-- req: R6.2 -->
### R6.2

Until it is written, the sprint-end issue (srd038 R1) summarizes the period and asks the owner.
