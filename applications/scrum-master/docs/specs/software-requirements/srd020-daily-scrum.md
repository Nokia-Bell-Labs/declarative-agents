## Problem

The morning is when a Developer decides what to do, and the decision needs
facts and two judgments. The facts are in the tracker: what is in
progress, what the overnight audits found, what is waiting and on whom.
The judgments are only in the person: what blocks them that the tracker
cannot see, and what interrupted them that the tracker does not hold. A
standup that asks for facts wastes the person's attention; one that never
asks leaves blockers and interrupts out of the tracker.

The daily-scrum skill shows the facts, asks those two judgments, and turns
each answer into an issue or a tracker write.

<!-- field: goals -->
## Goals

- G1: A Developer's morning is one command.
- G2: The command asks only about blockers and interrupts.
- G3: Every blocker and interrupt ends in the tracker, as an issue or a label.

<!-- req: R6 -->
## R6 - Direction

<!-- req: R6.1 -->
### R6.1

Each morning daily-scrum shows a person their in-progress issues, the overnight evidence findings on their Sprint Goals, and what is waiting, with a scored plan, and pushes an issue for each blocker or interrupt they name.

<!-- req: R6.2 -->
### R6.2

Until it is written, scrum-master standup (srd010) is the person's morning view.

<!-- field: non_goals -->
## Non-goals

- Team standups; the team view is scrum-master goals status.
- Reporting yesterday's work; the sprint update is a recurring audit (srd018 R6.1).
