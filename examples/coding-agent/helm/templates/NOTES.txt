The coding-agent topology is installed with three persistent role services:

  Planner:  {{ include "coding-agent.fullname" . }}-planner:{{ .Values.roles.planner.requestPort }}
  Executor: {{ include "coding-agent.fullname" . }}-executor:{{ .Values.roles.executor.requestPort }}
  Critic:   {{ include "coding-agent.fullname" . }}-critic:{{ .Values.roles.critic.requestPort }}

Each workload uses {{ include "coding-agent.image" . }} and mounts its prepared
role closure read-only at {{ .Values.profiles.mountPath }}. The image is
profile-free. All roles share PVC {{ include "coding-agent.workspaceClaim" . }}
at {{ .Values.workspace.mountPath }}.

This source chart requires `mage helmPrepare` before lint or install. Schema,
packaged-chart validation, and live deployment proof are delivered separately.
